# Nursing Research — Qualitative Research Proposal

**Course:** Nursing Research (2026)  
**Project Type:** Team-based Qualitative Research Proposal (8 members)

## Project Overview

**Topic: Experiences of GLP-1-Based Medication Use Among Normal-Weight University Students**

This project aimed to develop a qualitative research proposal exploring the experiences of normal-weight university students who had used GLP-1-based medications for weight loss or body management without a corresponding medical indication.

The proposed study focused on motivations for medication use, perceptions of benefits and risks, medication access, and decisions to continue or discontinue use.

## Research Design

The team developed a phenomenological qualitative research proposal using the following approaches:

- **Research design:** Phenomenological qualitative study
- **Data collection:** Semi-structured in-depth interviews
- **Data analysis:** Colaizzi's phenomenological method
- **Research rigor:** Credibility, transferability, dependability, and confirmability
- **Ethical considerations:** Informed consent, participant confidentiality, and institutional review board approval before conducting the study

This was a course-based research proposal. Participant recruitment, interviews, and qualitative data analysis were not conducted as part of this project.
