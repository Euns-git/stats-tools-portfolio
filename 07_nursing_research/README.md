# Nursing Research: Qualitative Research Proposal

Team project (8 members) for the Nursing Research course, 2026.

**Topic.** Experiences of GLP-1-based medication use among normal-weight university students.

## Project Overview

The team developed a qualitative research proposal on university students of normal weight who used GLP-1-based medications for weight loss or body management without a medical indication. The proposed study focused on motivations for use, perceptions of benefits and risks, medication access, and decisions to continue or stop. This was a course-based proposal; no recruitment, interviews or data analysis were conducted.

## Proposed Research Design

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Design | Phenomenological qualitative study |
| Data collection | Semi-structured in-depth interviews |
| Data analysis | Colaizzi's phenomenological method |
| Rigor | Credibility, transferability, dependability, confirmability |
| Ethics | Informed consent, participant confidentiality, institutional review board approval before the study |
